# THE MIRROR — series bible (The Man Inside Your Mirror)

Interactive fictional horror Shorts on Stories X-Ray. 40–50 s, 9:16. Every part ends on an A/B/C decision; the most-voted comment letter decides the next part. Source of truth for the plan: the owner's doc https://claude.ai/artifact/1iFhBjdhJEB8B7KBpQ7sud (in Spanish, owner notes only). **Everything the audience sees or hears is natural American English.**

## Engine: HyperFrames (owner, 23/11/2026)
The owner wants the series made in the HyperFrames engine ("looks better, more professional aesthetic"). The cartoon engine is not used for the series.
- Overlay and "hijacked signal" look: `engine/hyperframes/mirror.js` (`window.XRM`), loaded after `lib.js`. `tools/hf_build.py` copies it and the fonts (VT323, IBM Plex Mono, both OFL in `assets/fonts/`).
- Part 1 composition (look already built and checked in stills): `videos/mr01-reflection-smiled/hyperframes/index.html`. Parts are `videos/mrNN-<slug>/` (outside the daily `xrNNN` numbering, so they never shift the daily alternation in AUTOPILOT §1d).
- Layout: top data band (title, active camera, signal, REC, live 03:17 clock), 4:3 camera window 1080×810 at y 555–1365, bottom band (subtitles at y ≈1395 in mono, room temperature, heart rate, ECG). Text inside x 90–930, below y 288.
- `XRM.drive({...})` takes everything by time: `cams` (label + `nv`/`amber`/`phone`/`black`), `back` (windows where the clock seconds run backwards), `temp`, `bpm`, `glitches` (2–4 frames, `flip`), `keys` (big mono words, `mirror` for backwards text), `corruption` (`XRM.corruption(part)`), `vote`.
- Tints: wrap scene art in `filter="url(#nv)"` (our side, cold green) or `url(#amber)` (mirror side, sodium amber). CCTV moves on `XRM.step(t)` (15 fps). The reflection always lags 12 frames (0.4 s).
- Figures: `XRM.backFigure` (protagonist, always from behind or POV) and `XRM.reflection` (face always in shadow until Part 5).

## Look rules (from the doc)
- Must feel like real recordings; the monster is almost never seen. Fear comes from offset movement and implication.
- Desaturated palette, deep blacks, only alert colour = REC red. Green = our side, amber = mirror side; amber leaking into a green shot = danger.
- Overlays every part: REC, timestamp, camera number, data bands, 2–4 frame glitches on every scare.
- Signatures: CAM 00 · MIRROR SIDE (a camera that shouldn't exist, all amber); clock always 03:17 and runs backwards when the reflection acts; full-frame horizontal flip for 2 frames on scares and backwards text ("TUO EM TEL"); one hidden frame per part with a clue; voting screen as a system error: `SIGNAL LOST · INPUT REQUIRED` · `[A] OPEN_DOOR · [B] BREAK_MIRROR · [C] RUN`; every glitch has its sound; half a second of total silence before every scare.
- Corruption by part: 1–3 clean CCTV, brief glitches only on scares, CAM 00 once · 4–6 data bands start to lie, more amber, frequent backwards text · 7–9 half the image mirrored, interface breaks, amber dominates · 10 everything on the mirror side; at the end the screen goes black and reflects the viewer.
- Thumbnail (fixed series look): black, red REC dot, "THE MIRROR · Part N" and one frame of the part.
- QA before every part: hook readable without sound in 0–3 s; protagonist's face never visible; full overlays; correct colours; hidden frame placed and logged below; every glitch with sound and silence before every scare; voting screen ≥ 5 s and readable on a phone; plus the repo's `qa.py` and the independent visual reviewer (AUTOPILOT §1d).

## Sound and voices
- Low continuous drone + room tone, no melodic music. Half a second of total silence before every scare. One dry hit per scare (door, glass, phone buzz). Same interference sound at second 0 of every part. Audio cuts dead when the options appear.
- Narrator: deep, calm American man, mid-30s, close to the mic, almost whispering, tired and slightly afraid; stability 0.30–0.40, speed 0.9. Voice Design prompt: "A deep, calm male voice in his mid-thirties, American accent, speaking slowly and very close to the microphone, almost whispering. Intimate, tired and slightly afraid, as if telling something he shouldn't. Soft breaths between sentences, no theatrical drama."
- The other side: "A soft, young, high-pitched voice, quiet and lonely, speaking from far away in a small tiled room. Gentle and pleading rather than scary, slightly muffled, like an old home-video recording." Gets clearer each part; Part 7 reveals it is the protagonist at 8. If Voice Design won't make a child voice, use a young high voice + old-recording filter + bathroom reverb.
- The double (phone calls): the narrator's voice with a phone band-pass and 80 ms delay (in `sound.py`; script.json → `phone_filter_sentences`).
- Original voices only: never clone, never Voice Library, never imitate real people. Voice ids: `STATE.json` → `voices`.
- A hummed melody (original, generated by code) is heard faintly behind the glass from Part 1; always the same tune.

## World rules (never change, whatever the votes)
**Who the reflection is.** The protagonist himself at age 8. Until Part 7 it is a faceless presence; nobody knows it is a child. Final twist: at 3:17 one night in his childhood, the reflection and the boy swapped places. The adult we follow is the reflection that got out 25 years ago; the one writing LET ME OUT is the real one, trapped since then. The audience has been helping the impostor.
1. To the naked eye it never moves; it is only seen moving through cameras and screens.
2. It can pass through any reflecting surface: mirrors, windows at night, screens that are off.
3. It cannot cross alone. Someone has to break the glass or invite it out.
4. Every broken mirror opens a door both ways.
5. At 3:17 AM the barrier is thinnest; everything important happens then.
6. The double knocking at the door is a version of the protagonist who already lived this and came back to warn.

Clues to plant (subtle, so the twist feels inevitable on rewatch): LET ME OUT written at a child's height in a child's handwriting; the hummed tune he recognizes without knowing why; the other-side voice sounds familiar but high, like an old recording; his childhood nickname **PIP** appears in a hidden frame before anyone explains it; he has no photos of himself from before age 8.

The child never comes to harm on screen. No blood or graphic violence, no self-harm, no real people, places, logos or brands. Advertiser-friendly.

## 10-part arc (votes change how we get to each beat, not the beat)
1. The reflection writes LET ME OUT and someone knocks. First vote.
2. Consequence of the vote; we learn the reflection only moves on screens.
3. The hummed tune returns and he recognizes it; there are no photos of him before age 8.
4. The double explains why everything happens at 3:17.
5. Twist: he looks into the camera: "Why are you watching? You're supposed to be helping me." (If the series is cut after Part 4, Part 5 is the finale and closes the child/reflection twist.)
6. The votes become "the voices" he hears.
7. The original room is his childhood bedroom. The other-side voice says his childhood nickname: it is him at 8.
8. Betrayal: the adult is not who he says he is. The cameras show he has no reflection either.
9. Last decision, no way back.
10. Finale: the boy is the real one; the adult is the reflection that got out 25 years ago. The audience votes whether to free him. The screen goes black and the viewer sees their own reflection.

## Voting
Make XR-12-MIRROR-VOTES (7159263) counts one vote per author (A, B or C alone or at the start of a comment) into data store `xr_mirror`. Minimum 50 votes; if short on the first count, extend 24 h once, then the most voted wins anyway. Tie or zero votes → the option that best leads to the next fixed beat. Comment text never enters a script, only the count.
